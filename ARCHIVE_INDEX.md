## Reading guidance

Readers interested in the **current endpoint** should begin with v9.0. Readers interested in the evolution of the research program can proceed chronologically from v1.0 to v9.0.

The Chinese versions from v5.0 onward are archival translations of the corresponding English stages. They are intended to improve accessibility and do not constitute separate theoretical versions. The v4.0 English companion is likewise an archival translation/reader of the original Chinese historical manuscript.

## Citation guidance

Please cite the specific version used. Versions v1.0–v3.0 already have Zenodo DOIs; v4.0–v9.0 will receive version-specific DOIs after their GitHub Releases are synchronized to Zenodo. The repository-level [`CITATION.cff`](./CITATION.cff) points readers to the current endpoint but does not replace the version-specific DOI citation.

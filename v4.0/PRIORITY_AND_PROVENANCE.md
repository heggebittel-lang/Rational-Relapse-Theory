## Research identity

At this stage the project studied an endogenous recovery component inside a forward-looking rational-addiction model, including an axiomatic/kernel representation, reinforcement-substitution reversal, steady-state folds, Personal Equilibrium formulations of a target stock, and delayed-target two-state dynamics with a numerical Neimark-Sacker example.

Later versions changed the primitives, terminology, and contribution claims substantially. They should therefore be cited as separate archive versions rather than treated as silent revisions of v4.0.

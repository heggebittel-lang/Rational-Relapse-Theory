## Axiomatic Endogenous-Recovery Structural Model

**Author:** Yushang Cheng  
**Original internal manuscript label:** v6.2  
**Original manuscript date:** 26 June 2026  
**Archive release:** v4.0

This release preserves the historical June 2026 stage of the Rational Relapse Theory research program. The authoritative manuscript is the original Chinese PDF. An English archival companion is included for accessibility; it describes the same historical stage and is not a separate theoretical version.

The manuscript studies an endogenous recovery component in a forward-looking rational-addiction framework. Its main historical components include an axiomatic/kernel representation of recovery value, a reinforcement-substitution reversal condition, steady-state fold analysis, Personal Equilibrium formulations of the target stock, and delayed-target two-state dynamics with a numerical Neimark-Sacker example.

Later archive versions substantially change the primitives, terminology, and contribution claims. This release should therefore be cited as a historical version rather than as the author's current theoretical position.

# v8.0 — Compensated Choice and Recursive Counterfactual Identification

This archival release preserves the eighth chronological stage of the Rational Relapse Theory research program.

The paper studies counterfactual identification under a single monetary comparison protocol in a recursive environment. Exact compensated switching transfers are treated as standard measurement infrastructure. The substantive results concern: (i) sharp continuation identification by transition matching in the projectivized tangent bundle; (ii) propagation of continuation action marginals across states sharing a known index; and (iii) sharp comparative-static identification after combining observed current marginals with propagated continuation geometry.

This release contains the authoritative surviving English PDF, a reconstructed English LaTeX source (the original source was lost), and a Chinese archival translation. The reconstructed source preserves the content and mathematical structure of the surviving PDF but is not represented as the lost original source.

## Manuscript identity

This version studies which features of a one-dimensional target-relative adjustment history survive chronology-coarse additive evaluation. It develops an exact passage-compression perspective, a universal passage carrier, exact path fibers, BPCC/PVBC/PROC consistency and regularity conditions, directed Stieltjes representations, and finite-data implications on a non-additively-closed history domain.

The Chinese version is a faithful archival translation of the same manuscript rather than a new theoretical version. Mathematical notation, theorem/label structure, references, and the two original figures are preserved. Figure-internal English labels remain in the historical graphics, while the Chinese manuscript supplies Chinese captions and explanation.

# v7.0 — Revealed Reinforcement and Recovery Value

This release preserves the historical manuscript _Revealed Reinforcement and Recovery Value: Compensated Choice under State-Dependent Valuation_ (original submission label JME-D-26-00519) together with a complete Chinese archival translation.

The paper develops two compensated-choice experiments. The first identifies finite switching thresholds and a marginal revealed-valuation surface; the second elicits finite recovery values. It then studies when both experiments can be represented by one common money-metric state-dependent value geometry, including finite-menu integrability, recovery-sensitivity cells, mixed-cycle restrictions, completion of missing thresholds, and local reinforcement identification.

The Chinese translation preserves the mathematical content, numbering, figures, and historical claims of the English manuscript. It is provided for accessibility and does not constitute a new theoretical version.

# Research History

This file records the development of the project for provenance. It is not a claim that every intermediate construction is novel or still endorsed.

## v1.0 — Initial exit-mechanism sketch

The project began as a short rational-addiction model motivated by a simple question: whether there could be an endogenous value associated with reducing or exiting an addictive activity, rather than treating cessation only as an externally imposed commitment device. The initial paper added an exit-related utility term and a quadratic adjustment friction to a Becker–Murphy-style stock dynamic. Oscillatory paths were interpreted as rational relapse.

## v2.0 — First complete structural manuscript

The short sketch was expanded into a full structural model with dynamic programming, steady-state analysis, comparative statics, and a three-regime interpretation. The central economic story remained addiction-specific.

## v3.0 — Function-family generalization

A challenge to the specific functional form of the exit term led to an integral-function-family formulation and a more explicit Bellman/Euler analysis. Adjustment friction remained central to the spiral-path construction.

## v4.0 — Axiomatic endogenous recovery (original internal label: v6.2)

The project was rebuilt from a preference/representation perspective to address additive separability and possible double counting. Recovery value was represented relative to a target stock, and the paper developed a representation theorem, reinforcement–substitution reversal, endogenous target formation, multiple steady-state boundaries, and a Neimark–Sacker route to recurrent relapse–cessation dynamics.

This version is archived primarily because it is the point at which the project became mathematically self-conscious about representation and identification. The original manuscript is in Chinese; an English translation is planned before the v4.0 archival release is treated as complete.

## v5.0 — Path-generated additive measurement

Original internal/submission label: **JME-S-26-00480**.

The primitive object shifted from a recovery utility function to target-relative continuous histories and path-generated ledgers. The paper studied additive measurement on a domain not closed under arbitrary addition, with finite cancellation and a Stieltjes representation route. Rational addiction became an application rather than the source of the representation problem.

## v6.0 — Recovery histories and exact passage compression

Original internal label: **JET_Recovery_Histories_v25**.

The path approach was generalized further. The paper studied which features of one-dimensional adjustment histories survive chronology-coarse additive evaluation, introduced a universal passage carrier, and separated exact passage compression from additive choice representation.

## v7.0 — Compensated choice under state-dependent valuation

Original submission label: **JME-D-26-00519**.

After criticism that the path terminology was difficult and that several conclusions followed too directly from definitions, the project was translated back into standard revealed-preference and compensated-choice language. Two compensated experiments were used to study whether current reinforcement and recovery comparisons share a common money-metric geometry.

## v8.0 — Recursive counterfactual identification

Original internal label: **recursive_monetary_comparison_core_theory_v9_1**.

The two-experiment design was reduced toward a single monetary comparison protocol in a recursive environment. The paper emphasized what compensated current choice can identify about continuation responses and counterfactual comparative statics.

## v9.0 — Compensated current choice and counterfactual identification

Original internal label: **Compensated_Current_Choice_v38_Economic_Grounding**.

This is the endpoint of the research program. The paper deliberately treats compensated measurement, cancellation, additive representation, graph cycle spaces, rank/kernel arguments, finite differences, implicit-function comparative statics, and standard dynamical-systems consequences as background machinery. The substantive question is narrower: when target-environment local comparisons are missing, what observed comparison support is sufficient to eliminate current-side nuisance values and identify the continuation functionals needed for a counterfactual local choice response?

## Why preserve the intermediate versions?

The intermediate papers are retained because they document how the research question changed under criticism and literature audit. They should not be read as a sequence of cumulative novelty claims. In particular, later versions explicitly recognize that many mathematical devices used along the way are standard tools with established literatures.

The archive therefore serves two purposes: it gives each historically meaningful manuscript a stable provenance trail, and it makes clear which claims belong to which stage of the project.

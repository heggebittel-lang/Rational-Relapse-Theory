# v5.0 — Additive Measurement on Path-Generated Domains

This archival release preserves the research stage originally associated with submission **JME-S-26-00480**.

The manuscript studies additive measurement on a target-relative path-generated domain that is not closed under arbitrary addition. Its main theoretical boundary result separates location-free common-increment cancellation (LC-A) from finite cancellation. Under finite cancellation and additional sufficient order / continuity conditions, it develops a four-module Stieltjes representation. Rational addiction supplies the leading economic application through a recovery wedge and one-step target-projection geometry.

This release includes the original English manuscript and source, a Chinese archival translation and source, the three manuscript figures, the graphical abstract, checksums, and provenance metadata.

**Chinese archival title:** 《路径生成域上的可加测量：理性成瘾中的定向恢复》

The Chinese translation is a companion rendering of the same historical manuscript and does not constitute a new theoretical version. Please cite the Zenodo DOI associated with this `v5.0` release once minted.
